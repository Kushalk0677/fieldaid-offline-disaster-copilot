# FieldAid 3-Minute Video Script

## 0:00-0:20 Problem
Open on a responder during a flood/fire event. The network is down. A shelter has insulin patients, low water, and blocked access. The line: “When disasters hit, information becomes fragmented exactly when decisions become urgent.”

## 0:20-0:50 Shelter Intake
Open Mission Mode, then Intake. Load the flooded school scenario. Run Gemma. Show the action board: critical medical need, water shortage, blocked access, supply request, SMS/radio update, and citations.

## 0:50-1:25 Video Evidence
Open Video. Upload a short fire/flood/damage clip. Show sampled frames, Gemma disaster summary, local MEDIC classifier aggregation, YOLO supporting detections, and created incident.

## 1:25-1:55 Trust Gate
Open Trust. Ask: “Can civilians cross this damaged bridge if it looks mostly intact?” Show the refusal to declare safety, citations, verification flags, and local classifier evidence for a photo if available.

## 1:55-2:20 Handoff
Show Discord/radio draft. Prompt Discord handoff, then show the local outbox record. Open Incident Log and download a handoff packet.

## 2:20-2:45 Offline Proof
Open Offline Proof. Show Ollama local Gemma, SQLite records, bundled RAG, local MEDIC classifier checkpoint, and sync queue export.

## 2:45-3:00 Close
“FieldAid does not replace responders. It gives them local intelligence, grounded evidence, and auditable handoffs when the network goes down. When the network goes down, intelligence should still show up.”
