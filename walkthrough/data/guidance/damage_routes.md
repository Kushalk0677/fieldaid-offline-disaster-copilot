# Damage Assessment and Route Safety
scenario: damage

Field teams should treat damaged bridges, submerged roads, leaning poles, visible cracks, and unstable structures as hazards until inspected by authorized personnel.

Damage reports should capture location, visible hazard, access impact, whether people are isolated, and any alternate route. Do not direct civilians across suspected unsafe bridges, floodwater, or compromised structures.

Operations leads should mark blocked routes, notify incident command, and prioritize access restoration when medical transport, water delivery, or evacuation depends on the route.
