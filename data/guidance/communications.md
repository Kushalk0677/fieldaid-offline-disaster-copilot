# Offline Communications and Situation Reports
scenario: combined

Short radio or SMS updates should include urgency, location, affected population or asset, immediate need, route constraints, and requested next action.

Use plain language and avoid unsupported certainty. Distinguish observed facts from assumptions. When internet access is unavailable, keep a local log with timestamps so updates can be synchronized later.

Life-safety, medical, and structural recommendations should include an explicit request for human verification by the responsible authority.
