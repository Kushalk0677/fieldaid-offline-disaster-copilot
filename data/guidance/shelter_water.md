# Emergency Shelter Water and Vulnerable Residents
scenario: shelter

Shelter coordinators should maintain a current headcount, identify vulnerable residents, and prioritize drinking water, sanitation, medication continuity, and safe sleeping areas.

If water is limited, report remaining hours of supply, population count, and delivery access constraints. Dispatch decisions should account for elderly residents, infants, people with disabilities, and people who require regular medication.

Medical needs such as insulin storage should be escalated to qualified health staff. Local responders can help preserve access to cool storage and transport, but patient care decisions require a clinician or emergency medical authority.
